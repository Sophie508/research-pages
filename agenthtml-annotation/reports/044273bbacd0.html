Method & data

Two databases were queried read-only in Python. Region membership came from the exchange metadata; prices and dates came from the trade table.

Volatility definition

range = (High − Low) / Open × 100
  • Averaged per index over every qualifying trading day.
  • Filtered to Open > 0 and non-null High/Low.
  • Percentage — not points — so scale differences don't distort the comparison.
