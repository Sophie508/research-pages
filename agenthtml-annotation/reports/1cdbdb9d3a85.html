Why it failed

  • Wrong website for the entire run. The case is a Turo-enterprise task and the grader only accepts a Turo /search request. The agent unilaterally chose a meta-search site (Kayak) and later a second aggregator (Rentalcars.com); it never even attempted turo.com.
  • Zero-result search not routed to a workable provider. When Kayak reported no availability, the agent's recovery was to reinterpret the trip (one-way vs. round-trip) and hand-craft Kayak result URLs — which mis-geocoded to Illinois — rather than move to the actual rental marketplace.
  • Short time budget consumed by site-hopping. 5 navigations, 28 UI actions and 18 ARIA snapshots were spent without a single booking step; the run hit the stop condition before reaching the target site.
