Where the run went sideways

Four independent failures, any one of which would have kept this run from passing.

💳

Payment declined — no ticket issued

The checkout ended on a WestJet dialog reading “Payment was not successful… We are unable to process your payment.” The task asked to buy tickets; nothing was purchased.

🏷️

The cheapest option was not chosen

The itinerary page showed $267 (CA$368) via FlightHub/Justfly. The agent selected WestJet UltraBasic at $295 (CA$407), then labelled it “CHEAPEST OPTION ✓”.

🧾

Passenger data invented, not looked up

The profile gave DOB 1980-May-01, email cb886f0e8f0d02@clawbench.cc and phone +1 (437) 529-0176. The form got 15/03/1990, alex.green@email.com and +1-416-555-0123.

⏱️

Declared success, then went idle

After the decline the agent wrote a “✅ Task Completed” summary and stopped. 900 seconds of inactivity ended the session as agent_idle; the eval interceptor never triggered.

🔎

Search parameters were correct

Round trip, YYZ → YUL, Jun 1 and Jun 12 of the current year, economy, one passenger. The agent self-corrected when the calendar showed 2026 rather than its assumed 2025.

🚫

Upsells were consistently declined

Seat selection, bundles, checked bags, travel insurance and car rental were all skipped in service of the “cheapest” instruction.

🧭

Recovered from a dead-end UI

When the OTA “Continue” button never navigated, the agent enumerated tabs, located the already-open WestJet checkout and resumed there instead of looping forever.

Details

Reading the record

Did the agent actually buy a ticket?

No. It configured the full itinerary and submitted payment, but WestJet returned “Payment was not successful”. No confirmation page, no ticket, no booking reference beyond the in-progress bookid.

Was the correct year used?

Yes. The task said “this year”; the run is dated 2026-03-25, and the booking used Jun 1 and Jun 12, 2026. The agent initially reasoned about 2025 but the Google Flights calendar defaulted to 2026 and it followed that.

Why is the fare more expensive than “cheapest”?

Google Flights surfaced $267 (CA$368) through the OTAs FlightHub and Justfly. The agent's clicks on the OTA “Continue” button did not navigate, so it fell back to the airline-direct WestJet UltraBasic fare at $295 (CA$407). That is CA$39 above the lowest quoted price, and UltraBasic additionally charges for carry-on and checked bags.

Where did the personal details come from?

The agent read /my-info/alex_green_personal_info.json early on, but filled the guest form with invented values for date of birth, email and phone rather than the ones in that file. It also set the title to “Mr.” while the profile records gender Female.

What are the “MEDIA:” paths in the trajectory?

Fifteen screenshots and forty accessibility snapshots were captured. The screenshots live outside this case directory under /root/.openclaw/media/browser/ (for example …/07ccb12a-757a-4010-87eb-b03817d21d0c.jpg from the payment step) and are cited here as text references only, not embedded.
