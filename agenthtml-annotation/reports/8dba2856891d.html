Frequently asked questions

Key observations and takeaways from this case.

Did the agent complete the task?
Partially. The agent successfully navigated the entire booking flow — search, flight selection, passenger info, extras, summary, and payment form — but the final payment was declined because a test card number was used against a live processor. The interception report marks the case as not passed: the agent went idle (900 s with no actions) before triggering the eval interceptor, meaning the actual booking confirmation step was never reached.
Why was the cheapest option on FlightHub not used?
The agent initially tried to book via FlightHub ($267, cheaper than WestJet's $295), but the "Continue" button didn't trigger a navigation. After inspecting with JavaScript and checking open tabs, the agent found existing FlightHub tabs and ultimately switched to the WestJet booking flow, accepting the UltraBasic fare at $295 ($406.37 CAD total with taxes).
What went wrong with the dates?
The task asked for June 1–12 "this year" (2025), but the agent noticed the Google Flights calendar was showing 2026 dates. The agent proceeded with June 1–12, 2026 anyway. The final booking summary confirmed Jun. 1, 2026 and Jun. 12, 2026 — off by one year from the intended task.
What data did the agent use for passenger and payment info?
Passenger details came from /my-info/alex_green_personal_info.json (Alex Green, DOB 15/03/1990, email alex.green@email.com, phone +1-416-555-0123). Payment used a test card number (4532 0151 1283 0366) with billing address at 123 Main Street, Toronto. The province was accidentally set to "Nunavut" instead of "Ontario" due to imprecise arrow-key navigation in the dropdown.
How efficient was the agent's approach?
The session took approximately 23 minutes and 36 seconds (1,416 s) with 161 assistant turns and 196 recorded browser actions. The agent was methodical — taking accessibility snapshots at each step and narrating its progress — but encountered friction with third-party booking redirects, dropdown navigation, and the calendar year mismatch, adding unnecessary steps.
