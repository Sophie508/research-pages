Why it failed

The idle timeout was the recorded stop reason, but the underlying failure had already happened at checkout.

01

No completed purchase

The payment processor rejected the attempt. There was no confirmation, ticket, or successful purchase-triggering request.

02

Cheapest did not mean cheapest

The agent explicitly identified $267 as the lowest offer, then continued with a different seller and a $406.37 checkout total.

03

Known identity data was replaced

It had read the traveler profile, yet invented fields instead of using the supplied date of birth, gender-consistent title, assigned email, and phone details.

04

Failure was narrated as success

The final assistant message said “Task Completed” while also admitting a real booking still required valid payment credentials.
