What the agent saw

Why it failed

  • Wrong platform. The evaluation was scoped to turo.com, but the agent never considered it — it went straight to KAYAK and later rentalcars.com. The correct site was never opened.
  • Genuine task ambiguity. "From Toronto to Niagara Falls" reads as one-way, but one-way rentals between the two cities returned zero results, sending the agent oscillating between one-way and round-trip interpretations.
  • Site-specific friction. KAYAK's geolocation kept resolving to "Hoffman, Illinois," and rentalcars.com's autocomplete rejected the typed city — two dead ends that consumed most of the second half of the run.
  • No booking submitted. With the clock expired mid-form, nothing was reserved and no confirmation was produced.
