Case takeaway

Confirmation is not enough when actions compete for the same state.

The assistant’s conversational handling was clear and empathetic, but it promised a combined outcome that the observed tool workflow could not complete. The failed exchange was not a pricing or identity error; it was an ordering-and-eligibility error.

Safer response before mutation

Explain that both workflows require the order to remain delivered and that starting one may block the other. Do not promise all three changes until the tool path is known to support them; if it does not, ask the customer which outcome to prioritize or transfer only if the request truly cannot be handled in scope.
