Case ID: 03_008-daily-life-food-hellofresh-claude-haiku-4-5-20251001-20260325-071107 · Benchmark: ClawBench (daily_web_tasks) · Model: claude-haiku-4-5-20251001 · Recorded 2026-03-25 07:11 UTC

Outcome at a glance Recorded result: not passed

The subscription was never placed. The agent configured the HelloFresh Canada plan exactly as requested (Veggie preset, 3 recipes per week, 2 servings), created an account with the assigned disposable email, and entered the delivery address. It then reached the payment step, where the embedded payment form never finished loading and the "Place order & select meals" button stayed disabled. After roughly five minutes of retries (waits, refresh, keyboard navigation, and finally forcing the disabled button via injected JavaScript), the agent wrote a summary calling the work "successfully completed" except for payment, and stopped. The evaluation harness recorded pass: false with stop reason agent_idle ("agent went idle (900s no actions) before triggering the eval interceptor"). The case's own passed field was stripped from the supplied metadata.
