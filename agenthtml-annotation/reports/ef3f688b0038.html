Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Toronto reservations, pricing, and automated booking.

What is the 1-hour courtesy buffer at 63 Centre Ave. - Lot?
When you book a spot from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, SpotHero automatically reserves your access through 6:00 PM whenever permitted by the facility operator. This complimentary buffer ensures you don't face unexpected overstay penalties if traffic on Dundas or Queen Street delays your departure.
Why did the synthetic credit card fail during the autonomous agent run?
The test ran against SpotHero's live production web platform. The agent properly entered the synthetic credit card from the test profile (TD Aeroplan Visa ending in 4532), but live Stripe API gateway verification declined the fake card number. In real usage, any valid Visa, MasterCard, Amex, Apple Pay, or PayPal transaction succeeds immediately.
How do I access 63 Centre Ave. Lot upon arrival?
63 Centre Ave is a paved surface lot located just west of City Hall. Upon arrival, either show your digital mobile barcode to the attendant on duty or place the printed reservation pass on your vehicle dashboard. The pass includes your license plate (e.g., Honda Civic plate CQXW 234) for automatic plate recognition.
Can autonomous AI agents integrate with SpotHero for automated bookings?
Yes. SpotHero provides clean semantic markup, ARIA labels, and structured REST endpoints that enable next-generation AI personal assistants to search, compare rates, and complete parking bookings on behalf of users.
What is SpotHero's cancellation policy for downtown Toronto spots?
Reservations can be canceled for a full 100% refund up to the minute before your reservation start time (9:00 AM on March 28, 2026). Simply open your confirmation email or the SpotHero mobile app and tap "Cancel Reservation".
