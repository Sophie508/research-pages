Ali Khamenei, Iran’s Supreme Leader, was confirmed to have been killed in a U.S. and Israeli airstrike, but online-circulated photos of his corpse were revealed to be AI-generated.
According to Reuters and AFP on the 5th, a photo claiming to show “Khamenei’s body buried in concrete debris” began circulating on social media starting from the 28th of last month.
The photo depicts a corpse resembling Khamenei crushed under concrete debris. The corpse’s left little finger wears a ring Khamenei was known to wear regularly, and a turban—a traditional headgear worn by Iranian Shia clergy—lies nearby. Four rescue workers are seen lifting debris above.
This image was confirmed to be AI-generated. Google SynsID, a tool that identifies AI-generated images, analyzed the image with “very high” confidence as being created by AI tools. AFP’s analysis using other AI detection tools reached the same conclusion.
Additionally, the rescue workers in the AI photo differ from actual footage. While the AI image shows workers in black tops and bottoms with fluorescent yellow and orange vests, real rescue workers at the airstrike site wore red-colored uniforms. The corpse’s face shows no injuries, and upon closer inspection, the finger proportions are inconsistent—key indicators of an AI-generated image.
No official photos of Khamenei’s corpse have been released to date. While Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and U.S. President Donald Trump reportedly reviewed photos of the recovered body after the strike, they have not been made public. Although Khamenei’s body was scheduled to be shown to the public during his funeral, the event was postponed due to concerns over large crowds.
