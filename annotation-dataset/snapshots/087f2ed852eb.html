Veteran Tamil actor Sivakumar has been booked for defamation for condemning untouchability and the rich-poor divide followed in Tirumala Tirupati temple. A copy of the FIR, which was filed on April 29, 2020, has surfaced on the internet now.
V Subramanyam Reddy, vigilance inspector in the Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanam (TTD), has filed the complaint, stating that Sivakumar has made derogatory remarks and false propaganda against the practices in the Tirumala temple. The complaint further stated that Sivakumar's comments have hurt the sentiment of the devotees.
In the FIR, Subramanyam claims that he had received an email complaint from a person named Tamil Mayan on April 26, after he watched Sivakumar's fiery speech against the temple on YouTube.
Sivakumar has been booked under Indian Penal Code Section 500 (defamation), 505 (1) (B) (with intent to cause, or which is likely to cause, fear or alarm to the public, or to any section of the public whereby any person may be induced to commit an offence against the State or the public tranquillity) by the Andhra Pradesh police.

In an undated video, which is now going viral on social media, Sivakumar spoke about why he does not prefer going to temples these days. In the two-minute-long video, he condemned untouchability being followed in many temples.

One minute into the video, Sivakumar said, "A staunch devotee who had followed viratam (fasting) for 48 days reached Tirupati from Katpadi (in Tamil Nadu) by walk. He is made to stand in long queues and allowed to worship the Lord only after four days. The staff keep saying jarugandi jarugandi (move, move) and push him away. But, a rich person, accompanied by a mistress, comes and stays at the guest house. He consumes alcohol in the night and worships the Lord even without taking a bath in the morning. He is given complete respect with Kumbha Harathi. I am a witness to these incidents."
Citing another example, Sivakumar said that the sculptor who carved the Sivalingam in Tanjore Brihadeshwara temple is not allowed to touch the deity after kumbhabhishekam (a ceremony followed in Hindu temples to unite the powers of the Lord).
In 2019, Sivakumar was mired in controversy for knocking away a fan's phone who tried to take a selfie with him without his permission.
