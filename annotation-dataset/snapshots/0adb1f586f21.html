Vicky Kaushal
 and 
Katrina Kaif
 celebrated Mother’s Day in an earnest and candid way, just like their affable personalities. They both took to Instagram to share candid pictures, first with their respective mother and then with the mother of their life partner. 
Vicky
 shared three photos from his wedding to 
Katrina
 Kaif, which took place last year. In these three photos, Vicky can be seen dancing with mother 
Veena
, getting a kiss and blessings from her during a wedding ceremony and finally taking aashirwad by touching the feet, along with Katrina from her mother 
Suzanne
 Turquotte. Check out Vicky’s post below.
Katrina on the other hand, shared two pictures. First a candid photo with mother Suzanne, followed by a post-wedding photo where she is seen sitting with mother-in-law Veena and Vicky. She captioned her post, “Mother’s Day”. Check out her post below.
Captioning the intimate pictures with a poetic ode to mothers in Punjabi, Vicky wrote, “माँवां ठंडियाँ छाँवां. #HappyMothersDay”. Roughly translated, it means “Under the soothing shade of a mother”. Just a few days ago, Katrina celebrated her mother Suzanne’s 70th birthday with family and friends. She had shared candid photos from the small celebration and captioned her post, “Happy 70th Mama. May you always live life with the joy and courage that u do….. surrounded by your very noisy kids”. 
Vicky’s heartfelt wishes on Mother’s Day resonated with the fans, who found his Punjabi caption and the fact that he included Katrina’s mother in his post, very encouraging. Most fans commented how beautiful Vicky’s mother Veena Kaushal was looking in the pictures.
