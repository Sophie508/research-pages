An extremely rare pink platypus has been spotted in Australia, drawing global attention to one of the country’s most unusual animals. According to ABC News, Melbourne fisherman Cody Stylianou filmed the pale platypus in a river in East Gippsland, Victoria. The animal, nicknamed Pinky, stood out with its pink bill and feet, a coloration believed to be caused by albinism. Stylianou explained that he first saw the same platypus years earlier when it was smaller, but this was the first time he managed to capture clear footage.
According to Yahoo News Australia, albino or pale colored platypuses are among the rarest wildlife sightings in the country, with only about a dozen documented cases in the past 200 years. The unusual pigmentation is thought to be a genetic variation rather than an environmental effect, making each sighting scientifically valuable. Experts say that such rare individuals highlight the genetic diversity within species and the importance of protecting their habitats.
