About us
Anti Narcotics & Crime Control (ANCC)® is a non-profit organization dedicated to combating drug abuse, preventing crime, and rehabilitating individuals struggling with addiction. We work in collaboration with law enforcement agencies, government bodies, and communities to create a drug-free and safer Pakistan. Our Mission: Rescuing and rehabilitating drug-affected individuals. Spreading awareness about the dangers of drug abuse. Organizing campaigns, seminars, and awareness walks. Encouraging youth participation in social responsibility.
