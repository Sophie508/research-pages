State Flag Raised at Sukhbaatar Square
Society
Ulaanbaatar, February 18, 2026 /MONTSAME/. On the morning of the first day of the Lunar New Year of the XVII 60-year cycle, the Year of the Fire Horse, titled “Sureer Daragch,” the State Flag was raised at Sukhbaatar Square.
On this occasion, the National Anthem was performed by the Exemplary Brass Band.
