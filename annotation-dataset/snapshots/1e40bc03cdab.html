Egypt and China begin joint air force drill ‘Eagles of Civilization – 2025’
Saturday، 19 April 2025 - 08:22 PM
Egypt and China have launched a joint military air exercise, codenamed "Eagles of Civilization – 2025", involving multi-role fighter jets from both countries, the Egyptian Armed Forces said on Saturday 19/4/2025.
The drill is being held over several days at an airbase in Egypt and includes a mix of theoretical and practical training sessions.
According to the army, the exercise aims to align combat strategies, with joint air sorties and mission planning exercises forming a core part of the programme.
The training is designed to boost operational coordination and exchange expertise between the two air forces, while enhancing the readiness of pilots and crews for a range of combat scenarios.
The Egyptian military described the manoeuvres as part of broader efforts to deepen defence ties with China and strengthen joint military capabilities.
