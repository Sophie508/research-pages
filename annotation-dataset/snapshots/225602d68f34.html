Benjamin Taylor is a main production member and a co-creator of Fishtank. He has been a part of every season and spin-off of the show.
Overview[]
Ben started working with Sam Hyde and Million Dollar Extreme sometime in 2022 to 2023 seemingly as a production assistant. He moved from California (his home state) to Rhode Island for the job. Over time he developed a close friendship with Jet Neptune as coworkers and roommates. Little is known about Ben's activity during this time. He can be seen in the background working around the office in several Sam Hyde streams in late 2022 to early 2023 in the run-up to Fishtank's premiere. He also worked on audio for Million Dollar Extreme Presents: Extreme Peace. Ben was integral to the development and realization of the Fishtank concept, and has been a main crew member alongside Jet since the very beginning of the show.
During season 1, he became known for his on-screen appearances that included elaborate gaslighting, giving pep talks, and trauma dumping on the contestants. For season 2, Ben continued in much the same capacity as season one. One of his first appearances was competing in the Story Time Challenge, during which he was used as a bye competitor, and eliminated Trisha in the challenge by telling a story that mirrored her own. He was also present as a supervisor for the ejection of contestants like Cole and Jimmy. In Vampire Bloodgames, he appeared as an hot-tempered and insecure Sheriff Ben character most often bringing comedic relief. During season 3, Ben took on a flamboyantly gay persona as part of the Famous House facade. He spoke with a soft tone, enforced being politically correct, and sought to comfort the contestants. After the Fishtank reveal, he reverted back to his normal "straight" demeanor and was pointedly rude to the fish to sow confusion - this was shocking to several of the contestants. During season 4 Ben took on a similar role as he did in season one, he started a new bit where he would rub the back of contestants who were in distress, physically coddling them. On season 5 he dressed in golf attire, sometimes with a sport coat. He was a vocal supporter of James Drake, noting that he had personally cast James back during season 3.
In As Seen On, Ben unveiled a chic New York look- wearing designer jeans, a New York Times tote bag, Y2K style shirts, and a leather military-style cap (the signature piece of his look). He was often seen reading Milk and Honey, a collection of poetry by Indian-Canadian author Rupi Kaur.
Trivia[]
- Ben and Chris Lynch started hosting their own show called the Hong Kong 2 Podcast in August 2023, which regularly featured other personalities from Fishtank. In the Summer of 2025, Ben stopped appearing on the podcast to help the Fishtank crew with the move to a new state for Season 5. From that point on, Luke usually co-hosted the show with Chris Lynch.
- On January 25, 2026, Ben went viral on social media after a Fishtank fan named Wyatt posted an A.I. altered photo of Ben from Season 2 in a pink tutu, claiming it was a photo of Alex Pretti, a man who was involved in an incident with ICE that was a major news story at the time. Many different social media accounts believed the photo was real and reposted the image on their own accounts, where it gained millions of views before eventually getting community noted. In a livestream, Ben sarcastically thanked the community and Jet said he thought it was funny.
