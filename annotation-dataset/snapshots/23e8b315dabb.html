Israel poised to thrill on U-20 World Cup debut
Insight on team's coach, key player and one to watch ahead of maiden appearance at tournament.
- Israel were runners-up at last year's UEFA European Under-19 Championship
- Nation have qualified for FIFA U-20 World Cup for the first time
- Tournament to take place in Argentina between 20 May to 11 June 2023
Israel will head into the FIFA U-20 World Cup™ on the back of an exceptional performance at the 2022 UEFA European Under-19 Championship.
The team exceeded all expectations at the tournament in Slovakia, progressing all the way to the final where they were edged out by England after extra time. Having come so close to being crowned European champions, can Israel now cause shockwaves on the world stage?

Haim took charge of Israel U-19 in 2021 and led the team through the entirety of their European Under-19 Championship qualifying campaign and sensational run at the tournament finals.
Haim predominantly favours a 4-2-3-1 system. He likes his team to dominate possession and attack with speed and purpose. A former forward, Haim had a successful professional playing career in the Isreali leagues. He was the top-flight’s top goalscorer in 2003/04 during a prolific spell at Hapoel Be'er Sheva.
Midfielder Kancepolsky has emerged as integral figure for coach Haim, with his composure, tactical intelligence and quality on the ball helping Israel dictate the tempo of matches.
The 19-year-old also has an eye for goal and scored what proved to be the winner in last year's shock European Under-19 Championship semi-final victory over France.
Kancepolsky, who counts Spanish midfield maestros Sergio Busquets and Andres Iniesta as his football heroes, plays his club football for Hapoel Tel Aviv.
A strong, composed central midfielder with impressive tactical awareness, Madmon captained the Israel U-19 side at last year’s European Championship. Madmon’s mature performances and leadership qualities were recognised with a place in the Team of the Tournament.
Madmon plays his club football for Beitar Jerusalem – on loan from Hapoel Be'er Sheva – and has been a regular for the Israeli Premier League team this season. Madmon has been capped twice by Israel at Under-21 level.
