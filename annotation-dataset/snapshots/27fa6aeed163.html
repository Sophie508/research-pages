Serious question: Are we actually at risk of running out of fuel/petrol soon in Australia?
G'day everyone,
I've been seeing a fair bit of stuff online and on the news lately about fuel prices going mental and queues at servos because of the mess in the Middle East (Iran conflict, Strait of Hormuz stuff, etc.). Some headlines are screaming about "panic buying" and others saying our reserves are super low.
From what I've read in the last few days (Guardian, ABC, Reuters, etc.), the government (Chris Bowen) is saying we currently have:
~36 days of petrol
~34 days of diesel
~32 days of jet fuel
They reckon it's the highest it's been in 15 years and there's no immediate shortage because tankers are still coming and companies are meeting minimum stock rules. But then other places (farm groups, unions, opposition) are pointing out we're way below the International Energy Agency's 90 day emergency stockpile requirement (we've been non compliant since like 2012), and if imports got properly disrupted (e.g. longer conflict), things could get dicey fast especially for diesel which farmers and trucks rely on heavily.
I've seen reports of some regional farmers already struggling to get diesel deliveries, prices jumping 40c/L in spots, and worries about food supply chains if ag can't get fuel.
So serious question: Are we actually close to running out anytime soon, or is this mostly price panic and media hype? If the Middle East situation drags on for months, how screwed are we really? Anyone in logistics, farming, or the fuel industry got more insight?
Not trying to start a panic just genuinely worried about filling up the car and what happens if it gets worse. Cheers.
What do you reckon? Stock up jerry cans or chill? 😅
