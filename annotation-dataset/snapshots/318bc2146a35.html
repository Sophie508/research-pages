Thursday, Jan. 8, proved to be a memorable morning for Springfield’s Sigwo Arms owner Steven Grove.
Grove said he awoke to discover a series of individuals had attacked his personal Facebook page, claiming he and his business were connected to Immigration and Customs and Enforcement’s (ICE) fatal shooting of Renee Nicole Good during a confrontation in south Minneapolis on Jan. 7. Those personal attacks had spilled over to his business’ Facebook page.
Social media users have circulated the name “Steve Grove” as the ICE agent at the center of the controversial shooting. Multiple Reddit threads, Facebook pages and some news articles have circulated the name. Federal authorities have not confirmed the identity of the agent, only labeling him as an “experienced” agent.
Sigwo Arms owner Steven Grove said he spent all of Jan. 7 at his shop on Commercial Street or his home, and, of course, he has never worked as an ICE agent. He said he believes keyboard warriors took to Facebook to search for “Steve Grove,” found his account and misidentified him as the ICE agent involved in the Minneapolis shooting. The misidentification spread, and Facebook-owner Meta shut down his personal accounts.
“I never go by ‘Steve,'” Steven Grove said by phone Jan. 8. “And then, of course, I’m not in Minnesota. I don’t work for ICE, and I have, you know, 20 inches of hair on my head, but whatever.”
(From screenshots posted on the internet, the ICE agent believed to be involved in the shooting had short, dark hair.)
Steven Grove received Facebook messages threatening harm to him and his family and discrediting his business, located at 522 W. Commercial St.
“They’ve already started attacking the company (and) attacking me,” Grove said. “Somebody said, you know, I hope your family gets doxed.”
Grove posted some of the messages on his business’ Facebook page Jan. 8 in a post that was intended to alleviate the confusion. One of the messages stated “Stephen Grove is a murderer.” (Note that ‘Stephen’ is spelt with a ‘ph’ instead of a ‘v’.)
In the morning of Jan. 7, ICE agents were conducting an immigration enforcement operation in south Minneapolis. Good was sitting inside her vehicle when multiple agents surrounded the car demanding she exit the vehicle. When Good reversed the vehicle then pulled forward, videos and eye-witness accounts show an agent fired three shots into the windshield of Good’s vehicle. White House officials have said they believe Good was trying to run over an agent.
Good was later pronounced dead at the hospital.
Grove said he has appealed the shutdown of his personal Facebook to Meta, but Meta sent him a message saying he had violated community standards. As of 10 a.m. Jan. 8, his personal Facebook and Instagram account were still inactive. Sigwo Arms’ page was still operational.
Grove plans to continue to monitor both his personal and business social media accounts and appeal to Meta.
“Definitely a whirlwind morning,” Grove said.
