GOVERNMENT OF INDIA 
MINISTRY OF CIVIL AVIATION 
Rajya Sabha 
UNSTARRED QUESTION NO. : 2087 
TO BE ANSWERED ON THE  20th March 2023  
CONSTRUCTION OF AIRPORTS IN UTTAR PRADESH 
2087.    SMT GEETA  ALIAS CHANDRAPRABHA          
Will the Minister of CIVIL AVIATION be pleased to state:- 
(a) the number of airports proposed in the State of Uttar Pradesh; 
(b) the names of the cities in which these airports are proposed; 
(c) the number of airports where work is completed out of the proposed airports; 
(d) by when the remaining airports is targeted to be completed; and 
(e) the expected number of the airports including the international airport in the State of Uttar Pradesh 
after the construction of the proposed airport is completed? 
ANSWER 
Minister of State in the Ministry of CIVIL AVIATION      (GEN. (DR) V. K. SINGH 
(RETD)) 
(a) & (b): Government of India (GoI) has accorded 'In-Principle' approval for setting up of two 
Greenfield Airports in the State of Uttar Pradesh viz. Kushinagar and Noida (Jewar).  
 
 
(c): Kushinagar Airport in Kushinagar district of Uttar Pradesh has been operationalised in October, 
2021 
 
 
 
(d): The probable date of completion of Phase-I of the Noida (Jewar) Greenfield airport project is in 
September, 2024.  
 
 
 
However, the timelines for construction of airports depends upon various factors such as land 
acquisition, mandatory clearances, removal of obstacles, financial closure etc. by the respective 
airport developers. 
 
 
(e): There are total 19 Airports in the State of Uttar Pradesh including proposed Noida International 
Airport.  Details of these airports is attached as Annexure.
