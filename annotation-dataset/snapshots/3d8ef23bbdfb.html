Massive Russian strikes shut down Zmiiv and Trypilska power plants
Russia struck two thermal power plants operated by the state company Centrenergo — the Zmiiv thermal power plant in Kharkiv Oblast and the Trypilska TPP in Kyiv Oblast — forcing both to halt operations, the company reported on Facebook on Nov. 8.
The strike on the night of Nov. 8 was the heaviest since the full-scale invasion began, authorities said. Russian invaders used an "unprecedented number of missiles and countless drones," attacking the same power stations multiple times per minute that had been restored after destructive strikes in 2024.
"Not even a month has passed since the previous strike, and tonight the enemy hit all our generation simultaneously. The stations are on fire! We have stopped. Currently — zero generation. Zero!" Centrenegro said in a statement.
The company said all efforts to restore the power plants were completely wasted.
Before the full-scale invasion, Centrenegro operated three power plants. Russian invaders occupied the Vuhlehirska thermal power plant in Donetsk in July 2022, while two other stations were destroyed by attacks on March 22 and April 11, 2024.
In May 2024, the Ukrainian Cabinet of Ministers allocated 1.5 billion hryvnias to Centrenegro to restore the Zmiiv and Trypilska power plants.
In September, after another Russian strike, Serhiy Nahornyak, a lawmaker from the Servant of the People party and member of the parliamentary energy committee, said 19 drones hit the Trypilska plant, wiping out a year’s worth of restoration work.
In April, Centrenegro CEO Yevhen Harkavy said the company had lost 55% of its capacity.
Nov. 8 mass attack on energy infrastructure
During the night, Russia launched a new massive strike on Ukraine using 45 missiles and 458 drones of various types. Air defenses intercepted 415 targets, but 26 missiles and 52 drones hit their marks.
In Poltava, energy facilities were attacked, leaving the city of Kremenchuk without power, water, or heating.
In Dnipro, a drone struck a building, killing three and injuring 12.
Attacks were also reported in Kyiv and Odesa oblasts. In Chernihiv, an energy facility caught fire.
In Kyiv, a drone strike sparked a fire in the Pecherskyi district.
