Yantaiâ¯Penglai International Airport (IATA: YNT) is the main airport serving Yantai in Shandong Province, China, located about 43 km from the city center in Chaoshui Town, Penglai District. Opened on 28 May 2015 as the replacement for Laishan Airport, it features a 3,400 meter runway, an 80,000 m/s terminal, and serves as a focus city for China Eastern Airlines and Shandong Airlines. The airport handled over 8 million passengers in 2025, offering an extensive domestic network and international routes primarily to South Korea and Japan. Rated a 3-star airport by Skytrax, YNT continues to expand its facilities and route network as one of Shandong's key aviation hubs.
