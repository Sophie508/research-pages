BSEB Confirms STET 2025 Exam Date after Rumors of Postponement Spread Online
Clarification came amid confusion caused by BPSC TRE-4 delays, false alerts on some job portals, and speculation linked to Bihar Assembly elections, which is going to be held in November.
Bihar School Examination Board has issued clarification that Secondary Teacher Eligibility Test 2025 will be conducted as per schedule from October 14, 2025. This statement came in response to rumours caused by unverified reports of exam postponement circulating online.
BSEB has clarified that STET exam will begin on Oct 14 as announced earlier. "Bihar School Examination Board published Notification on October 1, 2025, in various newspapers, according to which the Secondary Teacher Eligibility Test (STET), 2025 is scheduled to be held from October 14, 2025. There is no change in the said date," BSEB tweet reads.
BSEB has also suggested aspirants to rely only on updates shared on its official website and verified social media accounts for authentic information.
Following clarification from BSEB, aspirants took to social media to share their feelings. One Ranjan Kumar wrote: "Thank you very much for the confirmation — confusion had been building since morning."
Prashant Kumar added: "Appreciate the clarification; it helps reduce stress before exams."

The Bihar STET 2025 exam was conducted from October 14 to November 16 on multiple days in multiple shifts. The BSEB will declare the Bihar STET result 2025 soon.
The Bihar STET result is prepared through the normalisation method as the exam is held in several shifts on multiple days. The STET normalised scores are the basis for the declaration of result.
