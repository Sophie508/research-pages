93-year-old grandmother did such a dance on Shammi Kapoor’s song ‘O Jaane Tamanna..’, seeing people said – there is no age to be happy
New Delhi :
A video is going viral on social media, in which 93-year-old grandmother is seen dancing with a lot of fun. She is seen doing a tremendous dance on Shammi Kapoor and Vyjayanti Mala’s song ‘O Jaane Tamanna Kidhar Ja Rahi Ho..’. In the video, you can see that other family members are also dancing behind them and supporting them. In the video you can see that she is wearing a nightie and has taken a shawl.
