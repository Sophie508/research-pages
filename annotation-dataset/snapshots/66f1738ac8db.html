Lightsky Whale Beam Lights up the Chongqing International Light and Shadow Art Festival
The Chongqing International Light and Shadow Art Festival recently concluded, leaving behind a trail of enchanting memories and captivating artistic marvels. The festival’s grand opening ceremony was illuminated by 160 Lightsky whale Beam lights, casting a mesmerizing glow over the event. The highlight of the ceremony was the breathtaking sight of two 240-meter-high twin chimneys, the tallest in Asia, adorned in a stunning display of light and shadow. Positioned in front of the chimneys was a colossal Mapping screen, representing the mountain city of Chongqing and showcasing a diverse array of products from China, Germany, Hungary, Spain, and other countries.
From December 29 to January 7, the Chongqing International Light and Shadow Art Festival features cultural activities such as light shows, photography exhibitions, fashion parades, and cultural and creative markets, attracting a large number of tourists to this City. Lightsky moving head light whale Beam, with its high-precision optical design and 200mm lens diameter, emitted a dazzling light that exuded a sense of modern technology.
Its remarkable ability to produce a full beam and sharp spot, combined with the use of USHIO NSLU5 lamps, ensured a long life of 4,000 hours and created a visually striking display with strong three-dimensional impact. Despite its powerful performance, it remained compact and lightweight, making it suitable for a variety of events, including concerts, stage shows, weddings, corporate events, parties, theaters, and car shows. Furthermore, its IP66 protection rating allowed it to be used outdoors even on rainy days.
The festival celebrated art, creativity, and technology, leaving an indelible mark on all who attended. It was a magnificent showcase of cultural diversity and innovation and everyone is looking forward to the next International Light and Shadow Art Festival and subsequent activities.
