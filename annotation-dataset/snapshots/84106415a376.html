Integration of Muslims into the Larger Social System in Kerala
Abstract
The paper begins with the evolution and spread of Islam in Kerala, a southern state in India. It goes on to explain how the Arab culture inter play with the existing culture in Kerala to form a mosaic culture. Historic, social, economic, political and cultural factors that led to the integration are discussed. Efforts have been made to understand the interplay of gulf migration, spread of communism, Kerala's geography, literature, art forms and mass media in the comparatively smooth journey of Islam in Kerala. The last century has seen some emerging factors that are leading to disintegration of the Kerala society. The paper ends with an open note for the reader.
