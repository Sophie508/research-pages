A speech made by U.S. President Joe Biden in 2020 has been clipped to make it appear that he endorsed Republican Donald Trump by saying, “we can only re-elect Donald Trump.” The short clip cuts the last part of Biden’s sentence, where he continues, “if in fact we get engaged in this circular firing squad here.”
Users are sharing a 14-second clip, in which Biden can be heard saying: “Turn, turn this primary from a campaign that’s about negative attacks into one about what we’re for. Because we cannot re-elect, we cannot win this re-election, excuse me, we can only re-elect Donald Trump.”
Examples of the misleading edited video can be seen (here) and (here).
Comments and replies on the posts indicate that many took the cropped Biden quote for an on-stage blunder made by the president.
One individual who shared the video said: “What a bumbling idiot” (here).
Another commented: “Anybody think he isn't suffering cognitive decline still?” (here).
Biden made the original speech at the WWI Liberty Memorial in Kansas City, Missouri in March 2020 (here).
In a livestream uploaded by FOX 4 News Kansas City on March 7, Biden’s comment can be heard in full (From timestamp: 38.19s): “Excuse me. We can only re-elect Donald Trump if, in fact, we get engaged in this circular firing squad here. It’s gotta be a positive campaign, so join us” (here).
The edited video previously circulated ahead of the November 2020 presidential election (here) and resurfaced after Biden officially announced his bid for re-election in 2024 on April 25 (here).
VERDICT
Missing context. The video clip crops Biden’s sentence before he specifies that Trump will be re-elected if Democrats get caught up in discord within their own party.
