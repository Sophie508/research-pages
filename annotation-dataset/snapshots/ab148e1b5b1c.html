This chart shows how much individual donors from this state have given in the 2023-2024 election cycle. Only itemized contributions of more than $200 are included.
- "Individual Donations to Candidates" shows how much was given by individuals to federal candidates
- "Individual Donations to PACs" shows the total hard and soft money given by individual donors to federal political action committees (PACs)
- "Individual Donations to Political Parties" shows how much individuals gave to national party committees or to state party committees registered with the Federal Election Commission.
- "Individual Donations to Outside Spending Groups" shows how much individuals gave to outside groups placing ads and engaging in activity to influence elections and public policy but not affiliated with any candidate or party.
