Dr. Peter McCullough revealed that mRNA vaccines were linked to 74% of examined post-vaccine deaths in a major autopsy study. Alongside top experts and officials, he challenged federal health agencies for downplaying myocarditis and other serious adverse events. This chilling testimony has ignited fierce debate, raising urgent questions about vaccine safety and transparency.
