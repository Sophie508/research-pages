Horror footage shows man throwing his wife off a ferry
CCTV footage has been released of the chilling moment a man is seen throwing a woman off a ferry, as passengers rush to save her. WARNING: Distressing.
WARNING: Distressing.
Shocking footage has been released, showing the moment a husband throws his wife off a ferry in Indonesia, as passengers rush to save her.
The chilling surveillance footage was released on February 24 by Indonesian police, and was taken from a ferry travelling through the Sunda Strait, The Sun reports.
In the video, a man can be seen walking up behind a woman as she is packing a bag. He then picks her up before tossing her overboard.
Miraculously, the woman manages to hold on and not fall into the ocean.
In the 40-second CCTV clip, he is seen hovering before making his move.
The couple walk over to some seats, and as the woman is preparing to sit, the man glances over at the ship’s railing.
He then removes her bag from her shoulder before picking her up.
The woman can then be seen kicking her legs as he walks closer to the edge of the ship before throwing her over the barrier.
The man then stands and watches, slumped over the railing.
Other passengers then swarm over to the edge of the ferry to try to help pull up the woman, who is clinging to the side of the ship.
Ridho Rafika, head of the Bakauheni Port Police Sector, said that the incident happened on the Shalem passenger ferry from Merak to Bakauheni.
He said that the victim miraculously survived as she managed to cling to the bars on the railing.
Local police said the man’s family did not report the incident as the husband suffers from a suspected psychiatric disorder.
It is alleged the man heard “whispers” that told him to drop his wife into the sea.
Mr Rafika said: “[The man] was suspected to be depressed because his reason was that he wanted to throw the victim into the sea; he heard a voice telling him to do that.”
The family has reportedly confirmed that the man is undergoing treatment. It is unclear if he has been arrested.
