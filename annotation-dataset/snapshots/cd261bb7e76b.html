Congratulations and Prayerful 
Wishes to our Dearest 
Archbishop Kuriakose Bharanikulangara
for his forthcoming  Episcopal Ordination day on 26th May
The whole diocesan community of Faridabad diocese expresses our sincere gratitude towards Very Rev. Dr. Joseph Odanat 
for his dedicated service in the diocese as the Vicar General.  Dr. Joseph Odanat has made an indelible mark in the history of 
the diocese of Faridabad. He worked hard and his experience at the time of the Delhi Syro Malabar Mission paved the way for 
the formation of many parishes. Later, during his fruitful tenure as the Vicar General of the diocese, he contributed a lot for the 
integral growth of the diocese. His presence was a source of inspiration for all those who worked with him, especially for the 
clergy of the diocese.   He was an ardent pastor and an innovative missionary who loved the Delhi Syro Malabar Mission and 
the new diocese with great zeal and vision.
Dr. Joseph Odanat was also the Chief Editor of the diocesan bulletin, Santhome Messenger of last three years.  His able leadership 
and talented pen helped a lot to present the Santhome Messenger with a new style and deep content. 
The diocese of Faridabad cannot forget the hard working and zealous pastor.  The diocesan community wholeheartedly thank 
him for his selfless service in the Eparchy and wish him all success and divine blessings in his future ministry.
Very Rev. Dr. Joseph Odanat

Doctor gives pills, but only God heals the 
patient. Dr. Sr. Fidelis SD knew this very well. 
She was a highly capable and competent doctor, 
who had secured both her Bachelors and Masters 
Degree in Medicine from the United States. 
Along with her professional competence, she 
carried the fire of faith within her. All those who 
had known Sr. Fidelis have borne testimony to 
her burning devotion to Mother Mary. Not only 
sisters, but also her fellow Doctors have witnessed 
miraculous healings taking place in the operation 
theatres because of Sr. Fidelis’ prayer through 
the intercession of Mother Mary. Sr. Fidelis was 
very fond of the prayer ‘Memorare’. She prayed 
this prayer in the operation theatre and delivery 
room, which had a soothing and healing effect on 
her patients.
Family is the best school of prayer. Sr. Fidelis 
learnt her loving devotion to Mother Mary 
from her father, Joseph Thaliath, who would 
daily recite Rosary with stretched hands. Sr. 
Fidelis followed the example of her father and 
went even further. She did not keep her Marian 
devotion limited to herself. She spread this 
tender devotion wherever she went. She used 
to organize a weekly Rosary procession with 
staff nurses in and around Jivodaya hospital. 
She was not ashamed or afraid of expressing her 
love for Mother Mary and proclaiming it before 
the patients and relatives. Many of those who 
travelled with her testify that she always started 
her journey with the Rosary, interspersed with 
Marian hymns.
The love for Blessed Mother planted in her 
heart in her family flourished and flowered when 
Sr. Fidelis encountered a great tragedy in her life. 
It was during the untimely death of her only SD 
companion, Sr. Christella in the foreign land of 
USA that Sr. Fidelis experienced the motherly 
accompaniment of Mary in a very special way. 
After Sr. Christella was taken away from her, 
Jesus gave her his Mother as her constant 
companion, with whom she developed an eternal 
relationship. In her moments of loneliness in USA

Diocese of Faridabad – Delhi Diocese, back in 
2012, missionary evangelization has been one 
of the thrust areas. The Ad Gentes mission has

Puthenveetil. Thus, due to many efficient

about the importance of learning a language.
