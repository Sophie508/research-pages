Police Inspects Prison To Control Gang Members
SAN SALVADOR, EL SALVADOR - JUNE 07: Police officers inspect the cells of Mariona Prison (formally called "La Esperanza") where gang members serve their sentences as part of Bukele's plan to combat crime on June 07, 2023 in San Salvador, El Salvador. Windows were sealed to avoid any contact between the inmates and other people outside of the prison. A group of inmates will be relocated to the mega-prison Terrorist Confinement Centre (CECOT) in Tecoluca, in the outskirts of the capital. (Photo by Handout/Presidencia El Salvador via Getty Images)
