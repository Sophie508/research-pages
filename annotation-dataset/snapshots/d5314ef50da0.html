Fuels Industry UK comment on fuel supply
Monday 16 March 2026
Fuels Industry UK spokesperson said:
"The UK situation remains stable with suppliers working hard to deliver normal levels of supply through a mix of UK refinery production and imported fuels.
Industry and Government remain in regular contact to monitor deliveries and stock levels in the UK as well as the situation internationally."
