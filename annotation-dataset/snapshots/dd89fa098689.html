Hi Woolies family,
We've come across a few concerning scams claiming to sell boxes of Woolies products for exceptionally low prices. The scammers share a link beginning with a survey, and ending with a request for card details - the URL redirects to different websites with each click.
The posts promoting this scam feature doctored images of Woolworths branding, and even have fake comments with "happy customers" who are claiming to have benefited from the "deal".
As always, we urge you to be mindful when shopping online and reach out to us directly if you have any concerns, or want to check the validity of a promotion. We are here to help.
Remember Woolworths only has one website: woolworths.co.za!
