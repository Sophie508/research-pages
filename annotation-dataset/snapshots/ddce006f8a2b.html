We will temporarily publish the latest available weekly statistics on fuel stocks held in Australia under the Minimum Stockholding Obligation, to support increased access to timely information.
Stock levels are reported weekly, with entities required to report stocks held on Tuesday by 11.59pm Fridays.

Quarterly and annual statistics
Statistics on total stocks held by industry under the Minimum stockholding obligation (MSO) are available to download. For an explanation on stocks reporting see Measures of liquid fuel stocks.
In 2025–26, the total industry MSO was set at:
- 1067 megalitres (ML) for gasoline
- 663 ML for kerosene
- 2742 ML for diesel.
In December quarter 2025, the total stocks held by industry under the MSO averaged:
- 1631 ML for gasoline
- 790 ML for kerosene
- 2905 ML for diesel.
The total stocks held exceeded the MSO. On average, gasoline stocks were 53% above the MSO, kerosene stocks were 19% above the MSO, and diesel stocks were 6% above the MSO.
At normal rates of consumption, on average in December quarter 2025 total stocks held by industry under the MSO were equivalent to:
- 38 days for gasoline
- 29 days for kerosene
- 32 days for diesel.
Compliance with the MSO is based on volume of stock held, not days.
The figures combine the different requirements for importers and refiners. For more information on the requirements for importers and refiners see Minimum Stockholding Obligation.
