1. T.T.V. Dhinakaran’s AMMK returns to NDA
Amma Makkal Munnetra Kazhagam (AMMK) led by T.T.V. Dhinakaran rejoined the National Democratic Alliance (NDA) led by the All India Anna Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (AIADMK) in Tamil Nadu. Mr. Dhinakaran met BJP’s election in-charge for Tamil Nadu and Union Minister for Commerce and Industry Piyush Goyal in Chennai and made the announcement.
Signalling a thaw in his equations Mr. Dhinakaran, AIADMK general secretary Edappadi K. Palaniswami welcomed the former’s decision. In return, the AMMK founder thanked Mr. Palaniswami for his favourable response. The day’s development marks the end of nearly nine-year-long stormy ties that the two leaders had.
2. OPS supporter Vaithilingam resigns as MLA, joins DMK
Former AIADMK Minister and sitting Orathanadu MLA R. Vaithilingam submitted his resignation from the Assembly, announcing his decision to join the DMK. He later joined the ruling party in the presence of Chief Minister M.K. Stalin at Arivalayam in Chennai.
A known supporter of former Chief Minister O. Panneerselvam, Mr. Vaithilingam is the second MLA aligned with Mr. Panneerselvam to switch allegiance to the DMK. Earlier, P.H. Manoj Pandian, MLA from Alangulam constituency had joined the DMK
3. Seven Mayiladuthurai fishermen arrested by Sri Lankan Navy
Seven fishermen from Mayiladuthurai district in Tamil Nadu were arrested by the Sri Lankan Navy last night for allegedly crossing the International Maritime Boundary Line (IMBL) while fishing in the Palk Strait.
CM Mr. Stalin wrote to Union External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar urging him to utilise appropriate diplomatic channels to prevent the arrests of fishermen from Tamil Nadu by the Sri Lankan Navy and to secure the immediate release of all detained fishermen and their fishing boats.
4. T.N. Assembly condoles deaths of former MLAs
The Tamil Nadu Assembly condoled the deaths of former MLAs Sa. Panneerselvam and L. Ganesan. Assembly Speaker M. Appavu read out the obituary references.
Panneerselvam was elected from the Tindivanam Assembly constituency in the 1991 election and L. Ganesan was elected from the Orathanadu Assembly constituency.
5. Don’t engage in mudslinging against judges: HC Chief Justice to ‘Savukku’ Shankar’s counsel
Madras High Court Chief Justice Manindra Mohan Shrivastava came down heavily on the counsel for YouTuber ‘Savukku’ Shankar alias A. Shankar for accusing Justice P. Velmurugan of being biased against his client in a petition filed by the Greater Chennai City Police to cancel the interim bail granted to him.
