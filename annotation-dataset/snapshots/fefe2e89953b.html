Unstoppable Huachicoleo: Stolen Gasoline is even sold in plastic bags
By Chivis 3/27/2019 11:57:00 AM
24 comments
Chivis Martinez Borderland Beat from T ribuna
P
uebla de Zaragoza,
Puebla.- After the Department of the Navy detained two people on board a car in
acting suspiciously, the elements discovered that in the trunk they transported
more than a thousand liters of gasoline in plastic bags.
In addition, a hose of
about 500 meters in length was found connected to a clandestine outlet and
along with it another 45 bags with hydrocarbons.
