Annotation Demo

What a VeriTaS claim looks like after our pipeline, with a human check on top: 20 claims, 11 text and 9 image. The claims and their fact-checking articles come from the VeriTaS export; the evidence statements and the link under each one are ours, extracted automatically, and every evidence item here carries exactly one source URL. Two people annotated in the interface without ever seeing the pipeline's verdict: they judged whether the claim is checkable at all, opened each source and flagged anything wrong with the statement or the page, then gave their own verdict. These 20 are the cases where a human verdict came out the same as the pipeline's, drawn from 27 annotated claims in total. On 2 image claims the two annotators reached different verdicts; both are shown and the card is marked. 64 evidence items are on this page and 11 of them were flagged for a problem — those are worth reading, since a matching verdict does not mean the evidence behind it was clean.
Annotator identities are not shown. The interface these annotations were made in is linked from the home page.
