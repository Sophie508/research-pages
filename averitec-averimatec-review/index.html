AVeriTeC + AVerImaTeC: the full splits through our pipeline

Every claim of AVeriTeC (train, dev) and AVerImaTeC (train, val) with its own claim and fact-check article as input. The pipeline decontextualizes the claim, extracts evidence statements from the article, attaches a source link to each statement (the extractor's citation first, then links in the article ranked against the statement, then a date-restricted search; a verifier labels every readable candidate and only statements with no candidate at all are dropped), predicts a verdict and is scored with the official EV2R protocol. AVeriTeC statements are merged to two before the verdict, as in the earlier runs; AVerImaTeC statements are not merged. Judge and extractor: Gemma 4 26B through OpenRouter. Every number comes from data/, built by build_review.py from the run's outputs.
