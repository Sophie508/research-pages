URL Grounding Review

Before and after, one evidence statement at a time. Half the evidence in the intake batch was grounded by searching the open web, because extraction returned those statements without a source URL, while the fact-checking articles they came from still held usable hyperlinks nobody looked at. Each row puts the URL the pipeline shipped beside the article link that replaces it, picked by shortlisting the three best candidates in the article and keeping whichever one verification accepts. Open-web search stays as a fallback: across the verified rows the two sources agree only twice, and each establishes statements the other cannot.
