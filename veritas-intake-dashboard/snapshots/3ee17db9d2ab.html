Drone attack sparks fires at Halliburton, KBR facilities in Iraq’s Basra
Basra (IraqiNews.com) – According to security sources, a drone attack targeted a facility housing foreign oil workers in Basra, causing fires at facilities owned by US corporations Halliburton and KBR.
The strike targeted buildings and warehouses utilized by enterprises in Iraq’s key oil-producing area, causing flames inside the property.
There were no reported injuries among employees during what Halliburton subsequently identified as a security issue.
Basra is Iraq’s oil sector hub, with several foreign energy companies operating there. Halliburton, one of the world’s major oil-field services corporations, has worked on initiatives to grow and modernize Iraq’s oil industry, including agreements to assist boost production at the Nahr Bin Omar oil field in southern Iraq.
The event occurs as strikes related to the regional conflict between the United States, Israel, and Iran have a growing impact on Iraq’s energy infrastructure.
Drone and missile attacks have recently targeted airports, energy infrastructure, and locations affiliated with foreign firms around Iraq.
The increasing assaults on energy plants and complexes housing foreign workers raise the possibility of Iraq being pushed more into the larger regional war.
