Charlie was shot and killed during his American Comeback Tour at Utah Valley University on Sept. 10 Erika Kirk accidentally used the word “grift” instead of “grit” while describing the work of her late husband, Charlie Kirk. Erika, 37, made the flub while she was honoring a student named Caleb at Turning Point USA's AmericaFest in Phoenix on Saturday, Dec. 20, video of the moment shared online shows. "Despite the devastating loss of Charlie Kirk, my incredible husband ... Caleb has persisted with the same grift,” Erika said, before stating "gift,” then finally, “grit.” “It has been a long day,” she continued, then turning to the student and adding, "Trust me, you're not a grifter, honey. It's all good." A grifter is someone who obtains something through dishonest means, while grit is used to describe passion and perseverance in relation to a goal.
