'Eroding Credibility...': NSA Doval Urges Swift Action Against Social Media Misinformation
Hindustan Times
328Likes
8.7KViews
2024Oct 26
On October 25, National Security Advisor Ajit Doval highlighted the escalating issue of misinformation on social media and its detrimental impact on the morale of the Indian Army. He called for timely and credible responses to false narratives, insisting that fact-based replies should be issued within hours of incidents to prevent misleading content from dominating public discourse. Doval noted the waning credibility of social media and urged efforts to expose blatant lies, particularly those that undermine the confidence of defence personnel in their leadership. He encouraged armed forces members to actively engage on social media as first responders, emphasising that prompt corrections could bolster national sentiment. Doval concluded by expressing faith in the many patriots and nationalists committed to defending the truth online.
