Election Fraud
Will doubts about electoral integrity impair the 2024 election?
- Report
Introduction
More than one-third of Americans, including a majority of Republicans, have little to no confidence in the voting system. Former President Donald Trump and other Republican officials continue to allege fraud and security risks, despite numerous studies and legal cases that found no evidence of widespread fraud in recent elections, and stricter laws have been put in place since the 2020 election. While election fraud occurred throughout American history, in contemporary times it is extremely rare. Democrats say safeguards are in place that ensure outcomes are legitimate. They argue fraud allegations are an excuse to put up hurdles that keep some from voting. Republicans counter there have been security breaches that dilute legal votes.
