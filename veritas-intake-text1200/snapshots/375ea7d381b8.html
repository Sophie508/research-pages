The claim that Bangladesh’s interim government has canceled holidays for Durga Puja, Janmashtami, Saraswati Puja and other Hindu festivals is false.
No holidays have been canceled. In 2026, schools will have five days holidays for Durga Puja, 1 day for Shibaratri, 1 day for Maghi Purnima, 1 day for Doljatra, 1 day for Laxmi Puja, 1 day for Shyama Puja and more.
Only those public holidays that coincide with weekly holidays (Friday and Saturday) were not counted as additional holidays.
For example, Shab-e-Meraj falls on 17 January, so it was not counted separately. Saraswati Puja falls on Friday, 23 January, so it was not mentioned separately. The same applies to Janmashtami and Ashura, which falls on Friday.
