Fly With Confidence -
Experience the Kenai Difference with Kenai Aviation's scheduled services
Embark on a journey where luxury meets reliability. Our experienced pilots, state-of-the-art aircraft, and commitment to safety ensures that you use Kenai Aviation’s scheduled service, you arrive in comfort & style, no matter the weather.
Officially launching in October of 2022, Kenai Aviation’s scheduled service has been connecting Anchorage to Kenai; providing an essential link to the peninsula by offering residents, workers, and tourists alike the safe and convenient option to skip the drive. With our state-of-the-art commuter aircraft, the Tecnam Traveller P2012, we are committed to making Kenai Aviation the premier airline for travel across Alaska and beyond. Join us as we expand our fleet and services to further our vision of becoming a trusted staple in the Alaskan aviation community.

Anchorage – Ted Stevens International
5000 W International Airport Rd, Anchorage, AK 99502
Our Anchorage check-in & service desk is located in the A-Gates on the ground level floor of the South Terminal at gate A11 past Baggage Claim 8 and down the hall.
All flights will board out of the right-most door behind our service podium in gate L2.
Arriving passengers can expect their baggage to be brought in by a ramp agent at gate A11 immediately after arrival.
