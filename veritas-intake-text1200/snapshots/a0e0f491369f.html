WB CM Mamata Banerjee Attends Event At Netaji Indoor Stadium
KOLKATA, INDIA - MARCH 23: West Bengal Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee during a programme for the widow pension scheme at Netaji Indoor Stadium, on March 23, 2022 in Kolkata, India. Speaking at the event Mamta said that the self-help groups (SHGs) would be given orders for stitching uniforms, providing them with employment. The clothes would be produced locally by small scale units and the money would also thus roll into Bengal. (Footage by Times Network India via Getty Images)
