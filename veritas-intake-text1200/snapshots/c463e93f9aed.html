Municipal elections of May 28, 2023 
Voting reciprocity agreements  
In the 2023 municipal elections, nationals of Bolivia, Cape Verde, 
Chile, Colombia, Korea, Ecuador, Iceland, Norway, New Zealand, 
Paraguay, Peru, the United Kingdom and Trinidad and Tobago who 
reside in Spain will be able to vote 
The deadline for registering in the electoral census is from 
December 1, 2022 to January 15, 2023 
The Electoral Census Office will send a communication to 455,024 
foreigners to facilitate this process 
 
On May 28, 2023, municipal elections will be held in Spain Residents of Spain, of legal age on 
voting day, who are nationals of Bolivia, Cape Verde, Chile, Colombia, Korea, Ecuador, 
Iceland, Norway, New Zealand, Paraguay, Peru, the United Kingdom and Trinidad and 
Tobago, having signed a reciprocal voting agreement with Spain in municipal elections.  
Those who wish to participate in these elections must request registration in the electoral 
census and comply with the other conditions of the Agreements. The conditions established in 
all, with the exception of the United Kingdom and Norway, are:  
- 
To have authorisation of residence in Spain.   
- 
Have resided in Spain, legally and without interruption, for at least five years prior to the 
application for registration on the electoral census.  
- 
Be registered on the Register of the municipality in which you habitually reside, which is 
where you can exercise your right to vote.  
The conditions set out in the agreement with the United Kingdom are: 
- 
Hold the corresponding residence permit in Spain.   
- 
Have resided in Spain, legally and without interruption, for at least three years prior to the 
application for registration on the electoral census.  
- 
Reside in the municipality in which you are entitled to vote and be registered in its municipal 
register.  
The conditions set out in the agreement with Norway are: 
- 
Hold the corresponding residence permit in Spain.   
- 
Have resided in Spain, legally and without interruption, for more than three years.

Registration to the electoral roll  
Nationals of the 13 countries residing in Spain who meet the conditions of the respective 
Agreement may request registration in the electoral census from December 1, 2022 to January 
15, 2023. 
To facilitate this process, the Electoral Census Office (ECO) will send a communication by post 
to 455,024 residents of Spain who are nationals of these 13 countries to request their 
registration in the electoral census.  
The ones who meet all agreement conditions will receive a password so that they can register 
for the electoral roll online. This procedure can also be carried out if if they identify themselves 
with Cl@ve. 
Those who meet all the conditions set out in the agreement except for the validity of the 
residence authorisation, must register in the electoral roll by post. They must provide a valid 
document that justifies authorisation to reside in Spain or proof that document renewal has 
been requested.  
Nationals of the 13 countries who have not received a letter and who meet the conditions of 
the Agreement, may apply for registration in the electoral census by going to the municipalities 
where they are registered within the period indicated above. 
Information can be requested at town halls, the provincial delegations of the OCE and by 
calling 900 343 232. 
Communications by country of nationality 
The countries of nationality with the highest number of communications to be sent are the 
United Kingdom (211,277), Ecuador (66,086) and Colombia (60,275).
